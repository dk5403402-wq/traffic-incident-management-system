Response Protocol


1)Verfiy the location and assess immediate danger to road users.


2)Set incident severity and notify the appropriate response team.


3)Coordinate lane controls, emergency access, and public updates.


4)Record actions and close the incident after the scene is confirmed clear.



Emergency:For immediate danger to life, contact local emergency services.
